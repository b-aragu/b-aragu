### Entrepreneurship & Business

Building businesses has taught me to think beyond the product itself. I work across sales, marketing, customer acquisition, business development, client relationships and commercial operations. ### Open-source AI & Generative Media

I enjoy getting hands-on with open-source AI models, exploring how they work, experimenting with different configurations and workflows, and discovering what can be created beyond conventional applications.

I'm particularly interested in generative media and multimodal AI:

- **Image generation:** Text-to-image, image-to-image and image transformation.
- **Video generation:** Text-to-video, image-to-video, video-to-video and visual transformation.
- **3D generation:** AI-assisted 3D creation, spatial content and interactive product visualization.
- **Audio generation:** Sound effects, generated audio, voice synthesis and sound design.

I explore open-source models and experimentation workflows through ecosystems such as **Hugging Face**, alongside creative tools including Google Flow, Google Omni, ElevenLabs and Artlist.

I particularly enjoy experimenting with faceless video creation, visual storytelling and connecting different models and tools into complete creative workflows, from an initial concept and script to generated visuals, sound and a finished piece.

There's something fascinating about being able to experiment with models, build on open-source work and turn an idea into something tangible without needing an entire production studio.

I'm interested in what happens when software, creativity and automation intersect.
